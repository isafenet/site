Udapt: The fitness plan that adapts around you.

Udapt checks each morning how you've recovered, from Apple Health and a four-question check-in, and fits today's workout, run, ride or swim and your food targets to it, as one connected plan. Every change comes with a plain-English reason and the research behind it.

Highlights

What Udapt does.

Go, Ease off or Recover

Your heart signals overnight and a four-question morning check-in, each compared with your own normal, never with other people's. No score out of 10, because no study gives one.

Training that eases off, not just logs

Weights go up when you're ready. A rough morning means fewer sets and a lighter effort, with the original session one tap away. Flag a shoulder, knee or lower back and exercises that load it are swapped like-for-like.

Run, ride and swim

A nine-week run/walk plan, running towards a goal or a race from 5K to marathon, easy rides, and pool or open-water swims, fitted around your strength training at your level.

Train anywhere

Gym, home (from dumbbells to a multi-gym), resistance bands, a hotel gym or just the floor: pick it for each session, at Beginner, Intermediate or Experienced.

Food targets that learn your body

The same calorie target every day, protein first. After a month of logging, Udapt learns what your body really burns. Log by search, barcode, or a photo or your voice with on-device AI.

Every change explained

Tap Why? for what changed and the research behind the rule, labelled by how directly it applies. No black box.

Made for Apple devices

One plan, not three apps.

  • iPhone: Today's readiness, training, runs, rides and food targets in one place, with spoken cues on every run and ride.
  • Apple Watch: A full companion app, included: the morning check-in, sets logged with the Digital Crown and your heart rate, and every interval of a run, ride or swim, with complications for today.
  • Widgets & Live Activities: Today's plan on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and your rest timer live during a workout.
  • Siri, Shortcuts & Control Centre: Ask for your readiness or log your usual breakfast, hands-free, or open today's workout from Control Centre.
  • Built with: Swift 6, SwiftUI, HealthKit, Core Location, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, App Intents, Foundation Models (on-device AI), Speech, WatchConnectivity and StoreKit 2.

Pricing: Udapt is a subscription: £6.99/month or £39.99/year, with a 7-day free trial on both plans.

Good to know: Udapt is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Always talk to your doctor about what's safe for you, especially around injuries.
