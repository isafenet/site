Our apps

Three apps, each for one real moment in your day.

AirReveal

Travel · iPhone & iPad

See what you're flying over.

  • Live flight map that works offline, using only GPS
  • Landmarks named as you pass, labelled on your camera in Window View, and the best side to sit
  • Landmark quizzes, Discovery Bingo, achievements and a country passport
  • A flight journal that turns your trips into a personal map of the world

GLPMGR

Health & Fitness · iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch

Your GLP-1 journey, tracked privately.

  • Dose reminders, dose steps (titration) and low-supply alerts
  • Injection areas taken only from each manufacturer's own leaflet
  • Weight, side effects, water, protein and blood glucose, gently
  • No account and no tracking: everything stays on your device

Udapt

Health & Fitness · iPhone & Apple Watch

The fitness plan that adapts around you.

  • A readiness score from Apple Health or a morning check-in
  • Training that eases off or progresses automatically
  • Food targets that shift with training days, plus photo and voice logging
  • Protects a joint by swapping out exercises that load it
  • My own plan: log the exercise plan your physio or rehab team gave you, exactly as written

How we work

Three words for every app we make.

Create.

We start with the person using the app and the problem in front of them, then design something calm and obvious.

  • Research and plain-language copy
  • Prototypes on real devices
  • Accessibility from day one

Ship.

We build carefully and test thoroughly, then launch with everything people need to get started.

  • Hundreds of automated tests
  • Listing, screenshots and website
  • User guides and support pages

Evolve.

After launch we keep listening and refining, and adopt new Apple features as they arrive.

  • Regular updates
  • New devices and OS releases
  • Your feedback shapes what's next

What we believe

Good apps treat people well.

People first

We write for real people: plain words, a gentle tone, and a user guide and jargon buster with every app.

Your data is yours

We don't run servers that collect your information. What you record stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Honest by default

Clear pricing, no dark patterns, and straightforward disclaimers where they matter, like health and travel safety.
