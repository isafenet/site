Our apps

Three new apps, made with care.

AirReveal

Travel · iPhone & iPad

See what you're flying over.

A window-seat companion that names the landmarks, cities and natural wonders below your plane, tells you which side to look from, and keeps a journal of every flight, even in airplane mode.

  • Live flight map that works offline, using only GPS
  • Landmarks named as you pass, and which window to look from
  • Landmark quizzes, Discovery Bingo, achievements and a country passport
  • A flight journal that turns your trips into a personal map of the world

  • iPad
  • Widgets
  • Live Activities
  • Offline maps
  • 6 languages

GLPMGR

Health & Fitness · iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch

Your GLP-1 journey, tracked privately.

A calm, private tracker for people taking GLP-1 medicines such as Mounjaro, Wegovy and Ozempic: doses and dose steps, reminders, injection sites, weight, side effects, water and protein.

  • Dose reminders, dose steps (titration) and low-supply alerts
  • Injection areas taken only from each manufacturer's own leaflet
  • Weight, side effects, water, protein and blood glucose, gently
  • No account and no tracking: everything stays on your device
  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Apple Watch
  • Mac
  • Widgets
  • Apple Health

Udapt

Health & Fitness · iPhone & Apple Watch

The fitness plan that adapts around you.

One adaptive Daily Plan that reads Apple Health each morning and adjusts today's training and today's food targets together, explaining every change in plain English.

  • A readiness score from Apple Health or a morning check-in
  • Training that eases off or progresses automatically
  • Food targets that shift with training days, plus photo and voice logging
  • Protects a joint by swapping out exercises that load it
  • iPhone
  • Apple Watch
  • Widgets
  • Apple Health
  • On-device AI

What we build

Native mobile apps, end to end.

From the first sketch to the App Store and every update after, built with Apple's own frameworks and design guidelines.

Native Swift & SwiftUI

Written natively in Swift, with SwiftUI, SwiftData and Swift 6 concurrency, so apps feel fast and at home on every device.

iPhone, iPad & Mac

One codebase that adapts: roomy layouts on iPad, a proper window on Mac, and a comfortable column on iPhone.

Apple Watch

Glanceable watch apps and complications for the moments when your phone isn't in reach.

Widgets & Live Activities

Useful information on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, updating live without opening the app.

Offline-first

GPS, maps and on-device text recognition that keep working in airplane mode or without a signal.

Private by design

No accounts unless they're needed, no advertising, no tracking. Sensitive data stays on the device and is encrypted.

Accessible to everyone

Dynamic Type to the largest sizes, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, clear contrast and plain, kind language.

App Store launch

StoreKit 2 subscriptions and free trials, App Store listings, screenshots, websites and user guides.

How we work

Create. Ship. Evolve.

Three words that describe how every iSafeNet app comes to life, and keeps getting better.

Create

We start with the person using the app and the problem in front of them, then design something calm and obvious.

  • Research and plain-language copy
  • SwiftUI prototypes on real devices
  • Accessibility from day one

Ship

We build carefully and test thoroughly, then launch with everything people need to get started.

  • Hundreds of automated tests
  • App Store listing, screenshots and website
  • User guides and support pages

Evolve

After launch we keep listening, fixing and refining, and we adopt new Apple features as they arrive.

  • Regular updates
  • New devices and OS releases
  • Feedback shapes what comes next
What we believe

Good apps treat people well.

People first

We write for real people, not engineers: plain words, gentle tone, and a user guide and jargon buster with every app.

Your data is yours

We don't run servers that collect your information. What you record stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Honest by default

Clear pricing, no dark patterns, and straightforward disclaimers where they matter, such as health and travel safety.

Always improving

Apps are never finished. We listen, refine and ship updates that make each app calmer and more useful.
